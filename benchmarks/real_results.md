## Size and compute

- Wall-clock of the 12-model sweep ÷ one Neurogenesis run: median **17.7×** (range 10.7–44.5×). In analytic FLOPs (incl. the audition) the ratio is larger, median 77×, because the sweep's big models dominate FLOPs but NumPy overheads dominate small-model wall-clock.
- Neurons chosen (1 layer): median 21, range 3–95. Random-candidate ablation: median 20. No-prune/fuse ablation: median 20.
- Does the width it picks track the validation-best 1-layer width? Spearman ρ = 0.34 (p = 0.275, n = 12).
